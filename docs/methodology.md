# Methodology

## Business Objective

Identify which Amazon products deserve promotion, pricing intervention, advertising support, and management attention.

## Analytical Approach

1. Clean raw product, price, category, rating, and review fields.
2. Validate schema and business rules.
3. Create decision features that describe value, popularity, trust, price competitiveness, and revenue opportunity.
4. Rank products and categories by executive priority.
5. Test whether discount strategy is associated with customer ratings.
6. Train a product success classifier using business features.
7. Recommend similar products with a content-based retrieval model.

## Limitations

The source dataset does not include true sales, margin, inventory, traffic, or conversion data. Revenue and success measures are therefore decision proxies based on price, ratings, discounts, and review volume.
